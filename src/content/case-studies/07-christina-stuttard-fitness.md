Christina writes her own workouts and changes them all the time. Her clients love the variety. Most of them are between 30 and 60, many aren't very technical, and they mostly look at her site on a phone.

Her old site was from 2014. It still listed services she'd retired, showed the wrong class times, sent people off to a quiet Patreon page and had blank spaces where videos used to be. She runs everything by text message, so anything she'd have to maintain was a risk.

> The question: what does someone who's just met her need to see before they send a message?
