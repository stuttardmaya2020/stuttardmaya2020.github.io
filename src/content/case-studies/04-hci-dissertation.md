It started during my year in industry. My manager had dyslexia, and writing alt text for our presentations showed me how much of what we shipped assumed everyone reads, sees and clicks the same way.

So for my final year, I tested that assumption in working software, not in theory.

> The question: how much does an interface have to change when the person using it does?
