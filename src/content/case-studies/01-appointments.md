"Virtual appointment" could mean video or telephone. So people prepared for both: travelling, packing bags, arranging childcare for a phone call. It was happening nationally, to people with low digital confidence and plenty of anxiety already.

> The insight: people didn't trust the notifications, so they compensated with stressful workarounds of their own.
