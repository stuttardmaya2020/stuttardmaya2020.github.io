# Surface brief: Home (and case study pages)

Scope: the whole portfolio. Visitor mode: Persuade. Replacement world (redesign), code-led.

Audience: design leads, recruiters, startups, all equal. Job: understand who Maya is in seconds, get pulled in, look at the work, reach her.
Kept content: case studies, paintings, photos, contact. Cut: about essay, values, experience list, fun facts, testimonials, FAQ.
Constraints: white ground with lilac hints (user-pinned); no terracotta; no hero scribble circles; far fewer words; no job-search language (PRODUCT.md discretion rule); client screens withheld.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a white-walled exhibition where product work and paintings hang on the same wall with small wall labels. It refuses the scrolling-resume portfolio (hero line, section after section of prose).

OWN-WORLD: Pure white walls, lilac tint mounts (#f6f3fd / #ece5fb) behind every exhibit, lilac vinyl-lettering accents (#5b3fb3 text-capable, #b8a4ec for lines), ink #17141f. One grotesk family (Schibsted Grotesk); hierarchy only from size, weight, case. Wall labels: small, left-aligned, title bold, then role/year, medium, one line. No grey text; no shadows; hairline rules only.

STORY: Visitor plays with a live piece, reads a four-line wall label that says who Maya is, walks the wall through five projects, sees the paintings and life photos hung the same way, and finds an email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Almost-empty white wall. Centre-left, a large hung canvas (about 60% width, 4:3, lilac mount) running a generative flow-line drawing that blooms around the pointer and takes paint on press. To its right, bottom-aligned, the wall label: "Maya Stuttard" / "Product designer & creative engineer" / "Untitled (you), 2026" / "Code, canvas and your cursor". Under it the primary action "Walk the wall ↓". Nav: name left, Work · Paintings · Contact right.

FORM: The White Wall, position 4 of my grounded list, seed key 067c0db3. Raises: one-voice type; no-grey text; hero answers the visitor's state; ordered next-exhibit navigation; each piece on its own flat lilac mount.

Signature interaction: the hero canvas. Idle: slow lilac flow lines. Pointer: lines bloom and bend around it. Press or drag: deposits deep-lilac paint strokes the flow lines route around. Reduced motion: one still frame, painting still works on press.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
